## #54 review fixes (on delize's branch, rebased onto main)

- Locked PDFs lost `/Info` and `/ID`: the decrypting parse garbles a trailer kept in a cross-reference stream. Now read from an `ignoreEncryption` parse first, restored after decryption; dead xref streams dropped, `/Size` recomputed. qpdf `--check` clean, `pdfinfo` shows title and author on RC4-40/128, AES-128/256 and classic-xref samples. Unencrypted sources are parsed once. Encrypted source without `/ID` reports `unreadable`.
- Luminance guard lowered from 0.3 to 0.05: it was swapping palette grey/blue and dark custom highlighter colours for yellow. Near-black (the #47 placeholder) is still swapped.
- Format 1 PDF/EPUB without `redirectionPageMap` maps `pages[i]` to source page `i`; notebooks never get a mapping.
- Build fails in production on any `require` of a non-external module (`findUnexpectedRequires`); fflate redirect resolved from the importer.
- Docs: annotated copy of a locked PDF is unlocked; highlight palette shades; re-annotating an already-synced document needs a manual sync.
