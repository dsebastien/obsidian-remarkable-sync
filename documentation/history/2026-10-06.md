# 2026-10-06: format 1 PDFs lose their annotated copy (#47)

## Accomplished

- Diagnosed #47 ("Annotated PDFs not synced/imported") against a live account. `.content` formatVersion 1 has no `cPages`; the page to source mapping lives in `redirectionPageMap`, positional against `pages`. `extractSourcePageMap` read only `cPages[].redir`, so every layer was skipped, `annotatedPages` was 0, only the original was written, and nothing was reported. 6 of 11 annotated journal PDFs in the test account were format 1, and exactly those 6 had no `(annotated)` copy.
- `extractSourcePageMap` falls back to `redirectionPageMap` when `cPages` is empty. Negative, missing or non-integer entries give no source page (inserted pages). Re-run on the real downloaded documents: all 6 now annotate, ink sits on the template lines.
- `renderAndWritePages`: when a PDF source has content pages but the annotator placed none, a Notice says so and `annotationsUnplaced` is set on the result.
- Business Rules and `docs/configuration.md` updated.

## Decisions

- `cPages` wins when both `cPages` and `redirectionPageMap` are present, matching `extractPageOrder`.
- No real device files committed as fixtures: the reproducing documents are personal journal pages. Specs use synthetic format 1 `.content`.
